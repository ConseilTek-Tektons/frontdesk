# Frontdesk support operations

## Email threading

Match In-Reply-To first, then References. Store every Message-ID once. If neither points to a known conversation, open a new ticket rather than joining by subject alone.

## SLA clocks

Calculate first-response and resolution due times using the policy business calendar. Preserve every pause and fulfillment timestamp for reproducible reporting.

## Routing

Evaluate versioned queue rules before agent assignment. Record the chosen queue and inputs, then balance only among active, eligible agents.

## AI assistance

Ground drafts in approved macros and readable ticket history. The assistant never sends messages, changes priority, or resolves tickets.
